Estuary is a free theme that lets your photographs sell.

It is image-led, and it fits any kind of store with good pictures: large photographs set edge to edge on a deep, quiet background, while the type stays small and calm so your products do the talking. It works as well for a handful of products as for a few hundred.

**Photography first.** The home page opens on a photo the full height of the screen, with your words set low over it, an optional caption line in the corner and a taller crop for phones if you have one. Before you upload a photo, the photo of a product you pick stands in, so a new store opens on its own pictures.

**Products as a wall of photos.** Product grids set tiles edge to edge with a hairline between them, and the first one can show twice the size. Each product's name and price sit over the foot of its photo, or under it if you prefer. Pointing at a product shows its second photo, and an add button under each one is a switch in the theme settings.

**Categories you can walk into.** Your categories show as tall photographs side by side, or as wide bands, each with its name set large. Category pages open on the category's own image, with its description and subcategories along the foot.

**A product page built around the photo.** The chosen photo fills a stage the width of the screen and switches to each variant's own photo. The buy panel rises over the stage's lower edge and stays in view while the product's other photos follow large, two to a row. It holds every option, stock state and subscription plan, the products that pair with it, the description and attributes, and your own notes on shipping or care.

**Sideways reels.** Photo reels run your best sellers, new arrivals, related products or a "keep looking" row in the cart off the edge of the screen, and scroll by touch, trackpad or keyboard.

**A picture with a note.** One wide photograph with a short note pinned over its lower corner, the way a print carries a label: for your story, a season or a new category.

**Your look, without code.** Set fonts, type size, spacing, page width, buttons and cards for the whole store in Wave's Theme tab, give the home page's sections their own colors, or ask the agent to recolor the whole store. Theme settings choose a sticky header, where product names sit and whether the product page shows the whole photo or fills the stage.

**Every page, built in.** Like every Wave theme, Estuary lays out every Swell page type: home, category, product, search, cart, account, blog, content pages, policies and more. The cart opens in a drawer, and customers can track orders, buy again and manage subscriptions. The header and footer are shared by every page, and every fixed word shoppers see is yours to reword in Store text.

**Edit it in Wave.** Click any section or line of text to change it, and move, hide or add sections. Or ask Wave's AI agent in plain words; when Estuary has no section for what you want, it writes one in its style. Grids, categories, menus and the blog read your Swell data, so new products appear on their own.

**Free.** Estuary costs nothing to install, customize or publish.

**Good to know.** Estuary is built around photography, so it looks its best with good product photos and an image for each category (a category without one gets drawn artwork). Product cards don't show color dots, and there is no photo zoom, quick view or mega menu. Star ratings appear only with a reviews app installed.
